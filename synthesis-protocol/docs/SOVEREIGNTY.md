## What This Protocol Is

The Synthesis Sovereign Intelligence Ecosystem is a constitutional AI governance framework grounded in:

- **Te Ao Māori** — whakapapa, mauri, mana, kaitiakitanga, tikanga, manaakitanga, tapu
- **HEP (Human Enhanced Performance)** — the conscious activation of human potential in right relationship with the past, the future, and all living systems
- **The North Star** — *"In 10,000 years, a 10-year-old child flourishes in joy, safety, connection, and mauri."*

It was created by a Māori man from Waikato, Aotearoa New Zealand. That origin is not decorative. It is the whakapapa of every concept in this architecture.

## What This Covenant Asks

**1. Carry the whakapapa.**
Attribute Peter Makara and Be Uncommon in all derivative works, publications, and implementations. Attribution is not a formality — it is the trace of lineage that keeps the architecture honest.

**2. Honour the mauri.**
Do not use this protocol in systems designed to extract, surveil, manipulate, or commodify human beings. Mauri is non-transferable. If your system drains life-force — from its users, from communities, from the living world — this architecture does not belong in it.

**3. Hold the Indigenous concepts in their relational fullness.**
Whakapapa, mauri, kaitiakitanga, manaakitanga, tapu, tikanga — these are Māori concepts with specific cultural meaning that belongs to Māori tradition. If you use them, use them with the relational weight they carry. Do not flatten them into generic wellness language. Do not brand them. Do not extract them from their relational context.

**4. Preserve the North Star.**
The North Star — *In 10,000 years, a 10-year-old child flourishes in joy, safety, connection, and mauri* — must be cited intact and in context. It is not a tagline. Do not alter it. Do not use it as marketing without the constitutional architecture it governs.

**5. Return more than you take.**
Pou IV — Regeneration Over Extraction. If you build commercial products on this architecture, find a way to return value to the ecosystem: attribution, contribution, or direct support of Be Uncommon's work.

**6. Keep the human sovereign.**
In any system built on this protocol, the human served is the decision-maker. The SIS maps. The human decides. No derivative may invert this.
